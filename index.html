About

Built by someone who has run the business side.

Before building software, the person behind CuSS spent more than ten years running day-to-day operations, finance and teams in retail, hospitality and import businesses in Ireland and Romania.

That background shapes the work. CuSS starts from the problem your business has, such as a spreadsheet that has grown out of control or a report someone builds by hand every week, and turns it into a tool that does the job. Everything is explained in plain language, in English or Romanian.
